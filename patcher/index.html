Make the firmware file for your Zoom L6.

This page turns Zoom's own L6 firmware v1.10 into the unofficial overdub / stereo AUX firmware, right here in this browser tab. Nothing is uploaded, and the page contains none of Zoom's firmware.

Download Zoom's L6 firmware v1.10

On Zoom's L6 support page, download L6 Firmware v1.10. You get L6_v1.10_E.zip; keep it zipped, you don't need to open it.

Before you install
  • This is unofficial firmware, not made or supported by Zoom. It changes your mixer's firmware.
  • If the mixer ever won't start after an update, you can put Zoom's own firmware back from the card. This has been tested with a deliberately broken firmware.
  • Zoom's terms don't allow changing its firmware without permission. Installing this is your decision and your responsibility.

3

Send it to the mixer.

Before you start: the mixer is switched on as usual with its card in and connected to this computer by USB, and ZOOM L6 Editor is closed. The browser will ask permission to talk to the mixer (it calls this "MIDI access"): allow it. Later it asks you to pick the card.

  1. aConnect to the L6
  2. bPut the mixer into File Transfer Mode The mixer pauses being a mixer and shows its card on this computer as a drive, just as ZOOM L6 Editor's File Transfer Mode does.
  3. cChoose the card and write L6.BIN Pick the card itself (its top folder holds RECORDER and SOUND_PAD). If the browser asks whether this page may view or edit files there, allow it.
  4. dEject the card, then take the mixer out of File Transfer Mode First eject the card: in Finder click ⏏ next to it; on Windows use Safely Remove Hardware. Then tick the box and press Leave.

The file is on the card. Now update the mixer (step 4).

4

Update the mixer.

  1. Keep the L6 powered from its USB port (batteries may not be enough).
  2. Switch the mixer off. If you took the card out, put it back.
  3. Hold PLAY/STOP, then press and hold POWER until the power light comes on; then let go.
  4. The power light blinks green slowly while it updates (about 30 seconds), then quickly when it's done. Don't switch off during the update.
  5. Switch the mixer off and on again.

If the power light blinks red quickly, the update failed: switch off, check that L6.BIN is in the card's top folder, and try again. If the mixer stays dark afterwards, it isn't broken: see If the mixer won't start.

First use
  • Overdub chain: hold EFX + PAN for 1.5 seconds. Both lights blink: on. Both light steadily: off. It starts off, and the mixer remembers it.
  • Stereo AUX: hold AUX 1 + AUX 2 for 1.5 seconds. Both lights blink: stereo (AUX 1 is the left side, AUX 2 the right). Both light steadily: two separate mono sends, as the mixer normally works.

With the chain on, each take you record moves onto pad 1 a few seconds after you stop, and older takes move along pads 2–4. Press pad 1 during the next take to layer over it. Keep the MASTER and SOUND PAD knobs at their 0 dB marks and the master compressor off, or every layer gets quieter or compressed again.

While the chain is on, the mixer keeps a tiny silent MASTER.WAV in each pad folder on the card; leave those alone. Pads in the chain all show the name MASTER.WAV; that's normal. Every take also gets a HISTORY.TXT listing what went into it. Switch the chain off whenever you want to keep your own sounds on the pads.

More in the full guide.

Going back

Copy the L6.BIN from Zoom's own zip to the top folder of the card (replacing this one) and update the mixer the same way. Your recordings, pad sounds and settings stay; the MASTER.WAV and history files can stay too.
