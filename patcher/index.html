Make the firmware file for your Zoom L6

This page turns Zoom's own L6 firmware v1.10 into the unofficial overdub/stereo AUX firmware. It all happens in this browser tab: nothing is uploaded, and the page contains none of Zoom's firmware.

1. Download Zoom's L6 firmware, version 1.10

On Zoom's L6 support page, download L6 Firmware v1.10. You get L6_v1.10_E.zip; you don't need to unzip it.

Open Zoom's L6 support page

Make sure it's the LiveTrak L6, not the L6max: the L6max has its own version 1.10.

Before you install

  • This is unofficial firmware, not made or supported by Zoom. It changes your mixer's firmware.
  • If the mixer ever won't start after an update, you can put Zoom's own firmware back from the card. This has been tested with a deliberately broken firmware.
  • Zoom's terms don't allow changing its firmware without permission. Installing this is your decision and your responsibility.

3. Copy the file to the card

  1. Put the L6's microSD card in your computer with a card reader, or connect the L6 by USB and click File Transfer Mode in ZOOM L6 Editor.
  2. Copy L6.BIN to the top folder of the card, replacing any L6.BIN already there.
  3. Eject the card properly before taking it out (or before leaving File Transfer Mode).

4. Update the mixer

  1. Power the L6 from its USB port (batteries may not be enough).
  2. With the mixer off, put the card in.
  3. Hold PLAY/STOP and press POWER, holding PLAY/STOP until the power light comes on.
  4. The power light blinks green slowly while it updates (about 30 seconds), then quickly when it's done. Don't switch off during the update.
  5. Switch the mixer off and on again.

If the power light blinks red quickly, the update failed: switch off, check the card, and try again. If the mixer stays dark afterwards, it isn't broken: see If the mixer won't start.

First use

Overdub chain: hold EFX + PAN

Hold both for 1.5 seconds. Both lights blink: on. Both light steadily: off. It starts off, and the mixer remembers it.

Stereo AUX: hold AUX 1 + AUX 2

Both lights blink: stereo (AUX 1 left, AUX 2 right). Both light steadily: two mono sends, as on a stock L6.

With the chain on, each take you record moves onto pad 1 a few seconds after you stop, and older takes move along pads 2–4. Press pad 1 during the next take to layer over it. Keep the MASTER and SOUND PAD knobs at their 0 dB marks and the master compressor off, or every layer gets quieter or compressed again.

While the chain is on, the mixer puts a small silent MASTER.WAV in each pad folder; leave those in place. Turn the chain off to keep your own sounds on the pads.

Going back to Zoom's firmware

Copy the L6.BIN from Zoom's own zip to the top folder of the card (replacing this one) and update the mixer the same way. Your recordings, pad sounds and settings stay; the MASTER.WAV and history files can stay too.
