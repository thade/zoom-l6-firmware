L6 QUEUED WORK OWNERSHIP AND TICKET TRANSPORT
3 October 2026

Follow-up: stream_producer_findings.txt records the next integration stage,
connecting whole-scan admission and ticket publication to original producer
instructions in the emulator. The modeled-producer limitations below describe
this earlier ownership suite; underlying RTOS delivery is still modeled in both.

Result
Added compiled ARM ownership tracking around the admission gate, plus ticket
encoding/decoding and an emulator connection to the original stream worker.
Each admitted job now has a unique ticket and an owned copy of its four scalar
arguments. A completion cannot retire a different or newer job merely because
they concern the same pad. Promotion-owned children keep the promotion gate
closed to unrelated requests until all children have finished.

This remains an offline integration prototype. Stock producer admission and
submission evidence are supplied by the harness; those producer call sites
are NOT patched. The underlying queue/semaphore scheduler is modeled. The
firmware image and mixer are unchanged.

The ledger occupies 780 bytes and holds at most 16 live entries. Each entry
contains its ticket, parent/child relationship, execution phase, submission
outcome, producer completion/wait-failure state, kind and four arguments.
Tickets increase monotonically and never wrap; exhaustion latches a fault.
Zero-initialize once before participants start. Resetting this ledger while
old work remains queued is unsupported.

The ledger uses a short atomic try-lock for its metadata. It never holds that
lock while invoking stock work or waiting for another task. BUSY requires
retry or retaining ownership and stopping; a caller must not ignore it and
continue mutating stock state. No device retry/latency policy is installed.
Its embedded gate must be used through the ownership APIs, not mixed with
unattributed calls to the older raw gate begin/end APIs.

Two independent completion obligations
Reserve before the first producer-side effect, then mark OFFERED before
publishing the message. The worker may claim and finish it before the producer
even receives its queue-submission result. An ordinary reservation retires only
when BOTH the producer has finished its cleanup and the worker has finished,
and no owned children remain.

Submission outcomes are explicit:
  ACCEPTED: worker completion is required.
  NOT_SENT: positive evidence that the request was never delivered; no worker
            completion is required, but producer cleanup still is.
  UNCERTAIN: retain ownership. A later real claim/completion can establish
             delivery; absent that, the job stays reserved.

A failed wait is recorded but does not complete the worker obligation.
Cancellation before publication can release a prepared reservation. That same
operation is rejected after publication may have begun. Claiming NOT_SENT after
the worker already claimed the ticket is contradictory evidence and latches a
fault rather than releasing the reservation.

The NOT_SENT outcome must not be guessed from an arbitrary error code. The
queue wrapper's return conversion was verified below, but the complete blocking
RTOS enqueue/failure contract is not claimed as proven by that conversion alone.

Children and promotion
An executing ordinary job or an exclusive promotion may reserve children.
Children inherit their parent's existing gate admission instead of opening a
new, unrelated admission. An ordinary parent that finishes before its children
remains reserved until the last child finishes. Promotion end returns BUSY
while children remain. Only an explicitly supplied, still-live parent ticket
can authorize this behavior; there is no global 'ignore busy' switch.

This gate excludes promotion from ordinary activities. It does not make all
ordinary activities mutually exclusive; existing firmware rules for recording,
USB/card access and playback must still be preserved.

Stale/duplicate/mismatched work
Public APIs reject retired tickets and duplicate state transitions without
decrementing another job's count. Worker claim checks kind and all four
arguments. A mismatch latches a fault before invoking the original stream
routine. A replay or attribution error encountered after dequeue fails closed:
remaining work stays reserved rather than being declared idle. Recovery from
that condition is not implemented.

Same-size stream queue transport
Original queue initialization 0x80048248 was executed with allocation and task
creation intercepted. The create call at 0x8004831c uses (8,16,0), and its result
is stored at 0x801f8f34: eight 16-byte messages for SamplerUpdatePlyStream.

The new proposed message is also 16 bytes:
  word 0: unique ticket
  word 1: format marker 0x4f445331
  words 2,3: zero
The original four arguments stay in the ledger. A compiled decoder validates
the format and ticket, then copies those arguments into the worker's existing
four-word buffer. It also sets a worker-local current-ticket fixture.

The emulator redirects two existing worker BL sites:
  0x80036c70 -> od_emulator_owned_receive: receive and decode the ticket message
  0x80036c7c -> od_emulator_owned_stream: claim, call original 0x800369a8, complete
The original worker loop and stream routine still execute. Queue delivery is
modeled. No stock image bytes are rewritten and these addresses are not an
installed patch plan.

Matching the old message SIZE does not preserve its old MEANING. Both producer
and consumer must change together, after draining old-format messages or using
a separately initialized queue. The decoder deliberately rejects old-format
messages; it has no fallback that could confuse a pad number with a ticket.
The worker-local ticket address 0x21003810 and ledger at 0x21020000 are emulator
fixtures, not recovered spare device RAM.

Correction to the preceding scheduling harness
The original low-level semaphore take 0x80076950 returns 1 on successful take
and 0 on failure/no token. This was verified by executing its original
available-token and empty/nonblocking branches against a synthetic semaphore.

In contrast, original queue-send wrapper 0x800483f8 returns 0 when its underlying
0x800763d8 call returns nonzero, and -1 when that call returns zero. It increments
its diagnostic queued count only in the former case.

The preceding sampler harness had modeled a successful wait as zero. That
fixture is now corrected to one; its failure cases now use zero. Re-running
the original producer/worker checks still demonstrates the important distinction:
successful wait returns 1 even when the injected file operation failed, because
it reports the wait outcome, not file success. This correction did not involve
the device. Earlier tests that ignored mocked mutex return values do not claim
real RTOS execution and are unchanged.

Verification
22 new groups pass, including:
- all 12 valid orderings of submission result, producer cleanup, worker claim
  and worker completion for accepted/uncertain submissions;
- out-of-order completion, duplicate completion, slot reuse and 116 distinct
  tickets without reuse;
- failed wait with pending worker work, definite nonpublication, unresolved
  publication, contradictory rejection, and prepared-only cancellation;
- kind/argument mismatches, capacity limit, sequence exhaustion and lock contention;
- parent completion before children and promotion-owned child completion;
- original stream worker with normal/early-cancel paths, replay rejection,
  mismatched payload rejection and inherited promotion ownership;
- compiled ticket decoder feeding the original worker out of order, malformed
  and old-format messages, original queue dimensions, and actual return conventions.

Ledger invariants are checked after API calls: unique live tickets, valid parent
references, correct child counts, and correspondence between root owners and
the embedded admission gate. These are deterministic emulator checks, not a
proof of arbitrary instruction-level preemption or device timing.

Storage pointed to by arguments is NOT copied by this ledger. Its current stream
payload is four scalar values; future path/buffer-bearing requests require owned
storage and verified lifetime. The ledger does not itself validate card readiness,
successful remount, pad reload, recorder finalization or audio correctness.

Next concrete integration
Reserve and encode at the real stream producer before pending-state changes
around 0x80036652, and connect confirmed enqueue outcome plus producer cleanup
to the ownership APIs. Preserve ownership on unknown enqueue/wait outcomes.
Audit direct prefetch callers so promotion-owned children receive their actual
parent ticket. Card-session ownership must still extend through verified local
readiness and pad-reload completion. Complete coverage, device placement and
firmware recovery remain required before a flashable implementation exists.
