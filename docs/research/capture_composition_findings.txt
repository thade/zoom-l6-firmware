Minimal capture composition — 9 October 2026
===========================================

Result
------
The previously separate minimal startup/hooks and external-history heap arena
now compose in one offline ELF. Cold startup observes the original initializer
result, attempts one allocation after stock idle-task creation, and registers
one optional worker. Success leaves W_WAITING, with audio/control hooks closed.
Allocation or task-creation failure preserves the stock startup continuation;
there is no retry, live free, storage release, file work or pad mutation.

The old contiguous, heap, segmented and fixed-address startup fixtures remain
separate. The composed fixture removes the old multi-megabyte guessed control/
history arena: only its permanent globals remain at a proposed gap address.
This is an offline integration milestone, not an installable firmware image.

Implementation and accounting
-----------------------------

The factory uses the original native allocator for all small runtime objects,
copies the eight-span metadata into that permanent allocation and retains the
allocation until reboot even if registration fails. Its local startup metadata
and WorkerConfig may disappear before the worker starts. There is no sample
clearing or access to external history during startup.

The MAIN proposal includes the same byte-gated startup capacity instruction
used in diagnostic 08. Existing native setup supplies both descriptors and both
cached capacities; the recorder does not repair or resize those live values.
The exact first eight tails remain listed in segmented_history_findings.txt.
The other four tails are not consumed. No tail ownership is inferred from this
build or from intact hardware guards alone.

The composed global proposal adds four bytes for the permanent arena pointer.
The prior profiles retain their original 96-byte limit. A fixture-only absolute
linker symbol selects the new bound; the linker rejects a larger global region.
Original scatter helpers zero the exact global span and expand both compressed
streams. All eight original scatter regions and MAIN/container lengths remain
in the proposal. This planner writes JSON only: no BIN, checksum, staging or
device operation. Recheck packing after every composed code change.

Offline evidence
----------------
Twelve new groups pass, exercising the compiled composed ELF, not the separate
heap or segmented research ELF:

* Every planned MAIN/DSP adapter survives minimal linking. The unused contiguous
  arena allocator and legacy pad/reload/SD recovery overlay do not survive.
* The complete original scatter entry expands the composed code and patched DSP
  and zeros 100 globals, preserving all original region contents and guards.
* The later original boot/scheduler path creates native queues and the optional
  arena using original allocator instructions. Task/mutex creation and IRQs are
  modeled; therefore the reported free heap is not physical device headroom.
* Registration copies descriptors/configuration and leaves the worker asleep
  without reading its manager or external history after the boot stack is erased.
  Startup cannot reach the public file APIs. The new globals' guards remain intact.
* Optional allocation failure (injected allocator return) and negative, zero or
  missing-handle task results stay closed and cannot retry. Failed stock task/
  mutex initialization or idle creation never allocates the optional runtime.
* Complete encoded control and DSP jumps preserve their stock continuations.
* Native FolderTree, FolderTreeSd and ExfatFoldersSd compositions produce one
  exact extra TMP and preserve all seven ordinary file ranges/payloads against
  a baseline. The exFAT case has 256-KiB clusters; this is a virtual geometry,
  not the measured on-card BPB. Astra recommended this final coverage case.
* A 2400-block take crosses multiple history wraps with repeated 400-block
  logical worker blackouts, continued audio after stop, successful catch-up and
  rearm. The same heap allocation preserves the preceding verified TMP.
* A 1100-block blackout loses required history, yields no eligible extra take,
  and preserves all seven ordinary files in the modeled storage backend.

Startup and recording are still distinct controlled harness phases: the full
scatter/boot tests do not run a whole interrupt-driven device boot, and the
recording harness explicitly authorizes worker release. The composed source
has no release caller. Native controller completion, scheduling, middle DSP,
task stack allocation and physical cache/DMA remain modeled or omitted.
Extra-file failure isolation in a synchronous backend does not prove ordinary
filesystem progress when a physical transfer retains shared resources.

Way forward
-----------
The composition prerequisite is closed offline; storage/device bindings remain
the next milestone. Do not turn mount status or Main-task identity into readiness.
Implement the smallest concrete storage admission/generation and transition
exclusion binding, with a physically justified completion/error join inside
live native frames. The first candidate can admit one storage generation per
boot and remain disabled after a transition until reboot; automatic recovery
is later work. The lease must also cover automatic manager rearm and the next
TMP creation. Qualify exact code/global and history alias/cache ownership.
Then fold copy-gap/history-age/loss/ordinary-progress and stack observations into
the minimal candidate, recomputing packing and heap margins. Verify effects and
master-dynamics separation at the extra tap. A short controlled hardware capture
and whole-song trial follow those bindings; pad publication follows capture.
