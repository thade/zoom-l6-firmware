Monitor output independent of MASTER (stock L6 v1.10, 18 October 2026)
========================================================================

A user asked whether the MONITOR output (headphones on the MONITOR jack)
could be fed from a point before the MASTER knob, so that turning MASTER
down for the room no longer turns the headphones down. On the stock mixer
the monitor level follows MASTER, and the MONITOR knob scales it further
without affecting the MASTER jacks (manual, "Adjusting the overall and
monitoring levels"). The question was whether that is wiring or software.

Static reading of the copied audio code (analysis/ram_audio_disassembly.txt),
MAIN (analysis/main_linear_disassembly.txt), the firmware's strings and the
manual's block diagram (Reference/manual.pdf, page 105). Nothing here was
emulated or tried on the mixer. Let B = 0x20010a48 and C = 0x20016f68, the
two audio state blocks.

Result
------
The dependence is in the hardware. The firmware produces one stereo master
stream, after the MASTER knob, the compressor and the master limiter, and
sends it to one D/A converter. That converter's output goes to the MASTER
jacks directly and to the MONITOR jack through the MONITOR knob, which is
an analogue control the firmware never reads. No firmware change can give
the MONITOR jack a different signal from the MASTER jacks.

The only other analogue outputs are the AUX jacks, which have their own
stream and their own D/A. A firmware could put the mix as it is before the
MASTER knob on the AUX pair, as a third AUX mode next to mono and stereo,
for headphones through an external amplifier (section 3). It is one
12-byte call site in the audio code, a copy of 512 bytes per block and the
mode handling; section 4 gives the effort. Without any change, the stereo
AUX send of 0.3.0 already gives a MASTER-independent stereo mix of the six
inputs on those jacks, without the pads, the effects and playback.

1. The master path, block by block
----------------------------------
The audio callback 0x2022a790 runs four stages for every 64-frame block:
0x20220ac8 (pad playback), 0x20224b90 (the channel mix into the master
blocks B+0x33b8 (L) and B+0x34b8 (R) and the AUX blocks B+0x35b8 and
B+0x36b8), 0x20220000 (the effects return, added to the master blocks) and
0x202263e0, the output stage, entered by a tail branch. In 0x202263e0 the
master blocks pass through these steps in this order.

  a. PLAY/STOP (0x202263fc-0x202269f6). The played take's blocks at
     B+0x37b8 and B+0x38b8 are multiplied by the gain B+0x61cc (ramp
     B+0x61d0, cursor B+0x61d4) and the live mix by B+0x61bc (ramp
     B+0x61c0, cursor B+0x61c4). When B+0x61dc is set, the played blocks
     are copied over the live mix (0x2022d2a0 copies words: source,
     destination, count). The three gains are set from MAIN at
     0x8000d376-0x8000d42c. This is the PLAY/STOP switch of the block
     diagram.
  b. MASTER (0x202269f8-0x20227134). Both blocks are multiplied in place
     by B+0x6214, per sample from a ramp when B+0x6220 points at one
     (cursor B+0x6224, loop 0x20226a18), otherwise by the scalar (loop
     0x20226b3c). Section 2 says where the value comes from.
  c. COMP (0x20227136). The master compressor, state near B+0x63f0, set up
     by 0x8000d4a0 (tap_processing_findings.txt).
  d. The master limiter (0x202278d8, enable B+0x62c0): the louder of L and
     R, one gain for both sides (aux_limiter_link_findings.txt).
  e. The two AUX limiters (0x20227bdc and 0x20227e5c) run on the AUX
     blocks here.
  f. The recorder copy (0x202280e0): both master blocks to B+0x1610 and
     B+0x1710 (0x20012058, 0x20012158), from where 0x2022a55a feeds
     recorder streams 10 and 11, the take's MASTER.WAV
     (uncompressed_tap_findings.txt). "REC DATA MASTER" in the diagram.
  g. A clip to the converter's range (0x202281c6-0x2022852a: per-sample
     compares in double precision with conditional stores).
  h. The USB copy (0x2022852c): both blocks to B+0x40c8 and B+0x41c8, the
     first two of the twelve channels that 0x20229538 interleaves for the
     computer ("PC IN MASTER").
  i. A soft mute (0x2022853e): a multiply by B+0x62f4 (ramp B+0x62f8,
     cursor B+0x62fc). 0x8000d290 sets it to 0 or 1 over 16 ms; it is
     called from 0x80006bd0, 0x8003c0a6, 0x8003f4c8 and 0x80048910, whose
     purposes were not traced.
  j. The meters (0x20228d08): the peak of each block held in B+0x6304 and
     B+0x6308 ("LEVEL METER").
  k. Conversion (0x20228df8-0x2022919e): each master block to saturated
     32-bit integers at 0x20010648 (L) and 0x20010748 (R). The AUX blocks
     follow (0x202291a0-0x20229536) into 0x20010848 and 0x20010948.

After the callback returns (0x8001078e), the audio task's block function
0x80010698 interleaves the integers frame by frame (0x800107a0-0x80010870):
0x20010648 and 0x20010748 into the buffer 0x80033510(0), 0x20010848 and
0x20010948 into 0x80033510(2). 0x80033510 returns 0x2000d4e0 for selector 0
and 0x2000d8e0 for selector 2, plus 0x200 for the second half (the byte at
0x801f9155). The DMA setup (0x8003318a-0x800331b0 and 0x8003323a-0x80033266,
transfer descriptions built by 0x8001ea40) moves 0x2000d4e0 to SAI1's
transmit data register (0x40384020) and 0x2000d8e0 to SAI3's (0x4038c020),
eight bytes per request (one frame of two 32-bit words) and 0x400 bytes per
transfer (two halves of 64 frames). SAI1's transmitter is set up for two
channels (0x80033150). These are the only two transmit streams the firmware
sets up. There is no third stream and no monitor buffer.

The block diagram agrees. In its OUTPUT box the master line runs through
PLAY/STOP, MASTER VOLUME, COMP and LIMITER, then the taps for REC DATA
MASTER, PC IN MASTER and LEVEL METER, then one D/A. The D/A's output goes
to MASTER L and MASTER R and, through MONITOR VOLUME, to MONITOR. A second
D/A serves AUX 1 and AUX 2 after their limiters. The specifications give
MONITOR as one stereo output on a TRS jack, 50 mW + 50 mW into 63 ohms
with 14.7 ohms output impedance (a headphone amplifier), against +15 dBu
and 147 ohms for MASTER and +9.5 dBu and 147 ohms for AUX.

2. Where the knobs enter
------------------------
The mixer has four knobs: SOUND PAD, EFX RTN, MASTER and MONITOR. The
firmware's event names include three knob events, EV_VR_SAMPLEPLAYER,
EV_VR_EFX and EV_VR_MASTER (strings at 0x800a2bcc, 0x800a2be3 and
0x800a2bbb; VR for variable resistor). There is no MONITOR event, no string
containing "monitor" or "phones", and no field in the audio state that a
monitor level could occupy, because every gain applied to the master
blocks is listed in section 1.

MASTER. Two dispatcher cases, both logging EV_VR_MASTER, handle the knob.
0x80040470 clamps the value to 0..127 (0x8000bf10) and calls the level
setter through the veneer 0x80002a88 with r1 = 0, which stores the gain at
once; 0x8003ecd0 does the same with r1 = 1, which ramps it over 16 ms
through 0x80010398. The setter 0x8000d5c0 maps the value through the
128-entry float table 0x8008b288 (index 127 minus the value): 0 at values
0 and 1, 1.0 at 87 (the 0 dB mark; start-up at 0x80006f86 and the reset
path 0x8002fa7c set 87), 10.0 (+20 dB) at 126 and 127. That matches the
manual's range of minus infinity to +20 dB. It stores the factor at
C+0x1f0, multiplies by C+0x1e4 and C+0x1d8 (both 1.0 after the bulk
recompute 0x8000d978) and writes B+0x6214. So MASTER is a digital gain in
the DSP, applied before the compressor, the limiter, the recorder, USB and
the D/A, which is why it moves everything downstream, the monitor
included.

MONITOR. The firmware never learns its position. The knobs it does know
arrive as panel events. The main chip's own ADC (ADC1 at 0x400c4000, set up
by 0x80032938 for five channels, 5, 4, 7, 6 and 3, from the table
0x80079f9c, and filtered by the CpuAdc task 0x8001de40 as a median of
three into a table at 0x80570d34) is read by 0x8001c002, 0x8003005e,
0x80021052 and a diagnostic dump at 0x8001f800. None of these writes the
audio state; what they measure was not identified (battery or USB power,
by the neighbouring event names). The converters are configured over SPI
(the audio device init 0x80032c20 calls the LPSPI init 0x80028858). The
I2C bus (wrappers 0x80032b40 and 0x80032bb0) reads one device's registers
2, 8, 0x41, 0x46, 0x4b, 0x6b, 0x74 and 0x75 at start-up and from
0x80048480; nothing on either bus takes a knob value. The MONITOR knob
therefore acts after the D/A, in the analogue headphone path, as the block
diagram draws it.

So the dependence is wired. The master D/A's output is one signal. The
MASTER jacks get it as it is and the MONITOR jack gets it through the knob.
Whatever the firmware puts in the master stream appears at both.

3. What a firmware can do
-------------------------
Three options, in rising effort.

  a. No change. Keep MASTER at the 0 dB mark, as the README asks for
     overdubbing, and set the room level on the PA or the powered
     speakers; MONITOR then sets the headphones alone. This is how the
     stock design is meant to be used: MASTER is a mix level, not a room
     level.
  b. The stereo AUX send of 0.3.0. In stereo mode the AUX pair is a stereo
     mix of the six inputs, placed by pan, following the channel levels
     and mutes (post-fader sends), independent of MASTER, COMP and the
     master limiter, on two line-level jacks. A headphone amplifier on the
     AUX jacks gives MASTER-independent headphones today. Missing from it:
     the pads, the effects return and PLAY/STOP playback, because the
     sends are taken from the input lanes, and each channel's AUX 1 send
     has to be set (the unity marks give the mix as the channel levels
     have it).
  c. A monitor feed on the AUX jacks, as a third AUX mode. At 0x202269f8,
     before the MASTER gain, the firmware copies the two master blocks
     over the two AUX blocks. From there the stock code treats the pair as
     AUX: the AUX limiters (linked in stereo by 0.3.0's routines), the
     conversion, the interleave, SAI3 and the AUX D/A. The jacks then carry
     the complete mix, the inputs with EQ, level, pan and mute, the pads
     with their levels and the SOUND PAD knob, the effects return with EFX
     RTN, and the played take when PLAY/STOP is on, without MASTER, COMP
     or the master limiter, at a fixed level. The listening level is set
     on the headphone amplifier.

The rest of this section is about option c.

The site. The 12 bytes at 0x202269f8 are movw r0,#0x6220 (4 bytes), ldr
r0,[r4,r0] (2), cmp r0,#0 (2) and beq.w 0x20226b3c (4). They become movw
and movt r0 with the routine's address, blx r0 and a nop, as the two
limiter sites did (plan_master_ref.py LINK_SPECS; recipe format 2 carries
such edits as span.dsp_edits). The routine, in monitor mode, copies 0x200
bytes from B+0x33b8 to B+0x35b8 (L and R are 0x100 apart on both sides,
so one copy covers both), then replays the three instructions: load
B+0x6220; when it is zero, continue at 0x20226b3c (the scalar gain loop),
otherwise return to 0x20226a04 (the ramp loop). r4 holds B throughout the
stage. r0-r3 and ip are free at the site, because both continuations begin
by writing r0 and neither reads the flags. 0x202269f8 is the target of the
cbz at 0x202269d2 and the site begins exactly there; no branch lands inside
it. In mono and stereo the routine does the mode test and the replay only.

The mode. A third value of the AUX mode bytes CONFIG+0x30d/0x30e (00 00
mono, A5 5A stereo; 5A A5 would do for monitor), saved with the settings
like the others. A release that does not know the value (0.3.0 tests for
A5) reads it as mono. The stereo filter in stereo_aux.c can treat monitor
as either mode: the AUX gain rows no longer matter, because the copy
overwrites the AUX blocks after the mixer loops have run (they still run,
which is harmless). The limiter routines' STEREO_OR test (master_hooks.S)
must accept the new value so that the two AUX limiters stay linked on the
copied stereo mix; otherwise the image shifts while one side limits
(aux_limiter_link_findings.txt). The switch is an open choice: either the
AUX 1 + AUX 2 hold cycles mono, stereo, monitor, with a third LED pattern
to confirm it, or a second same-bank hold (bank 2 holds HIGH, FREQ, MID,
LOW, AUX 1, AUX 2, EFX and PAN; EFX + PAN is the chain's). The requester
and the owner should decide.

Cost per block. One 0x200-byte copy, 128 word loads and stores, once per
64-frame block of 1.33 ms, the same size as the USB copy stock already
makes at 0x2022853a in the same stage, plus a mode test; in the other
modes, the mode test and the replayed instructions. Code: one routine of
about 30 instructions, a third 12-byte DSP edit, and the mode handling in
stereo_aux.c, panel.c and master_hooks.S.

Interactions.
  - Take chain and recorder: none. The recorder copy at 0x202280e0 is
    downstream of the site and unchanged, so MASTER.WAV still contains the
    knob, COMP and the limiter, and the README's advice to keep MASTER and
    SOUND PAD at 0 dB and COMP off while overdubbing stands. The monitor
    feed makes the advice easier to follow, because the listening level no
    longer has to come from MASTER.
  - Stereo AUX: monitor mode replaces the stereo send. There is one AUX
    pair, so the two cannot be had at once.
  - Limiters: the master limiter is not in the monitor feed, because it
    sits after MASTER; the AUX limiters are, at the same threshold.
  - COMP: pre-MASTER is also pre-COMP, because the stock order is MASTER
    then COMP. A compressed but MASTER-free monitor is not available from
    the stock order.
  - Level: the AUX jacks' maximum is +9.5 dBu against +15 dBu at MASTER,
    so the same mix comes out about 5.5 dB lower on the AUX jacks than at
    the MASTER jacks with MASTER at 0 dB, if both converters reach their
    maximum at digital full scale, which no experiment has established.
  - The alternate audio callbacks (0x800124d9, and the ones 0x8000ca98 and
    0x8000cab0 install) apply the MASTER gain in MAIN (0x80012b30,
    0x80016890) and would not be covered, as they are not by the limiter
    link; which modes they serve is not established
    (audio_task_and_taps_findings.txt).

4. Effort, risks and the bench
------------------------------
Emulator. One suite on the model of tests/emulation/verify_aux_limiter.py,
about six groups: the plan changes only the three DSP sites; the output
stage 0x202263e0 run whole on synthetic master and AUX blocks
(verify_tap_processing.py already runs the full master body), where in
monitor mode the AUX blocks at the limiter entry equal the master blocks
as they were at 0x202269f8 and the master output, the recorder copy and
the USB copy are stock bit for bit; in mono and stereo everything,
registers included, is stock; the ramp and the scalar continuations both
taken; the recipe carries three edits and the browser patcher rebuilds the
image (verify_patcher.py). The switch and the third mode value go into
verify_stereo_aux_hooks.py's panel group and verify_master_everyday.py.

Build. A mode in tools/firmware/build_master_trial.py, as experiment 41 was
built before it became the release.

Bench, with the setup of experiments 30 and 41 (USB tones into input 5,
the AUX jacks into the Syntakt, tools/device/l6_aux_measure.py). In monitor
mode, MASTER turned from 0 dB to minus infinity and to +20 dB must leave
the AUX level unchanged while the MASTER jacks follow; a pad and PLAY/STOP
playback must appear on the AUX jacks; COMP on must not change them; mono
and stereo must still give experiment 41's figures. The MONITOR jack can
be recorded the same way with a TRS-to-two-TS cable into the Syntakt's two
inputs, which would show it following MASTER on stock and on the new build
alike, and would give its level against the AUX jacks for choosing a
headphone amplifier.

Risks. A third edit to the DSP record, where the project has two. A third
value in the mode bytes the panel toggles, with an LED pattern to confirm
it. A user in monitor mode loses the AUX sends until switching back. Line
level into headphones needs an amplifier. The alternate callbacks above.

Effort. One note (this), one emulator suite, one trial build and one bench
experiment, then a release: the way experiment 41 went from note to
measurement in a day. The switch and its LED pattern add panel work and
hooks-suite groups on top of that.

5. What was not checked
-----------------------
Nothing was emulated or measured for this note. Not identified: the
converter chips, the SAI frame format beyond two words per frame, the I2C
device, what the main chip's ADC channels measure, the four soft-mute
callers' purposes, and the alternate callbacks' modes. The block diagram is
Zoom's drawing, not a schematic; the conclusion rests on it agreeing with
the code, which has one master stream and no monitor control anywhere.
