STOPPED HANDOFF: ADMISSION AND COMPLETION MAP
5 October 2026

Result and decision
There is no single verified stock pause flag or queue acknowledgement that
supplies BackingGate.enter. The capture-only control drain intentionally keeps
ordinary commands working, so it is not the missing device gate. A dedicated
handoff admission boundary must cover the independent domains below while their
existing work continues to completion. Do not reinstate the old broad ownership
overlay merely to obtain a success response.

The immediate design remains opportunistic: if pads, recording, a reload or
card/USB activity are still in use, defer the optional handoff. Do not force-stop
a playing pad, stop the audio task, suspend completion workers, or acquire a
filesystem/scratch lock as the gate. Existing admitted stop/completion work must
remain runnable. All entry coverage and matched completion evidence below are
requirements before the current unbound gate can be implemented on the device.

The inventory pins the v1.10 SHA256, decodes the main region at 0x80001000 and
the startup-copied 0xd6dc-byte region at 0x20220000, and lists 35 entries with
86 direct branch/call references after adding the three-argument pad command
roots (see pad_commands_findings.txt). Seven callback/worker boundaries are recorded
separately. Linear disassembly is an audit aid, not exhaustive coverage: data
may resemble instructions, indirect stores/calls are not resolved, and absence
of a direct reference is not evidence of absence of a caller.

1. Pad controls and rendering
Entry candidates: outer control handlers 0x8003efb0 and 0x8004b828; actual
start/restart helper 0x80002c60. The bounded direct-call inventory reaches that
helper at 0x8003efd8, 0x8003f016 and 0x8004b864. Guard before its stop/disable,
seek and re-enable mutations, but preserve the outer command's semantics.

New original-instruction probes show both outer handlers ignore the start
helper's return and continue to 0x80008430 with the pad number for injected
returns 0, 1 and 0xffffffff. A fabricated BUSY return at the helper would not
defer the original command. Both handlers can instead call stop 0x80002d08
when the inspected mode/playing conditions select it. Blanket blocking of
the whole handler family could prevent a needed stop. No user-facing names
for the handlers or LED/status helpers are inferred from these addresses.

Completion: retain ownership through start/restart's seek and the entire playing
interval, including gaps between refills. After stop/natural EOF, require a
matching whole audio invocation to have returned plus no queued/running refill
or file descendants. Common AudioProcess call/return are 0x8001078c/0x8001078e.
A completion acknowledgement alone does not exclude the next callback; starts
and relevant mode changes must remain closed. The normal and alternate pad
renderers are both relevant. Existing native_renderer_fence tests demonstrate
these relationships only under their modeled stop/queue/control contracts.

2. Background refill and sample-file workers
Registered callback 0x800366f0 -> scan 0x80036508 runs from the copied-RAM
AudioSubProcess dispatcher 0x2022a7a8. Admission must precede scan mutations,
not only the send at the publication path around 0x8003664a. Prevent NEW scans
while allowing accepted refill/read work to complete.

Refill queue: 0x801f8f34; worker 0x80036c60; call/return 0x80036c7c/0x80036c80
around 0x800369a8. Sample-file queue: 0x801f8f30; worker 0x80036118; read
0x80036184/0x80036188, seek 0x800361bc/0x800361c0. Completion includes actual
status/count, error handling, signal tail and the waiting producer's return.
An empty queue can coexist with an executing job. A signal or cleared native
pending field cannot retire an uncertain/undelivered request. Initial handoff
reads use the new synchronous callback; this does not remove ownership needs
for pre-existing ordinary refills, reads or seeks.

3. Recorder and transport
Higher-level entries already observed by capture transport:
  0x80034f40, 0x80034e58, 0x80034d18, 0x80035038, 0x800350d8.
Their packet families include record callback 0x8004b9a0, alternate transport
callback 0x8004b890 and stops 0x8004ba40/0x8004b910. There are inline send paths;
intercepting only producer 0x8004b6d8 is incomplete. Queue 0x801f8f38 belongs
to RecPlayCtl 0x80034bc8, with callback call/return 0x80034c20/0x80034c22.
Direct backend/forced-cleanup paths also need coverage.

Begin ownership BEFORE the original request's first mutation or enqueue, retain
the session through audio producer/file/flush work, all finalizers and closes,
postprocessor 0x800032b0 including reopening tail 0x800035d0, and enclosing
callback return. A post-file-drain checkpoint at 0x8000b78c precedes these closes
and later reopens. It is not sufficient for handoff admission. Close errors
remain uncertainty even when the recorder subsequently clears its state.

A new regression runs the CURRENT compiled capture drain: its marker is
acknowledged successfully while a newer stock transport callback 0x8004b891
remains queued. This is intentional capture-only behavior, not a new regression.
The handoff must independently prevent new recorder sessions. Never wait for
RecPlayCtl to become idle from inside the very callback it is executing.

4. Pad assignment, catalogue and deferred reload
Entry 0x80008c68 mutates before queueing; mode 2 clears synchronously. Other
modes put a borrowed path pointer in a 32-byte packet. Deferral needs owned
arguments or a preserved synchronous call, not a shallow packet copy.

Catalogue scanner 0x800082c0 has independent direct callers; directory/card
setup can scan before deferred reload producer 0x80008d68. That producer sends
callback 0x80049da0 to queue 0x801f8f3c. Worker call/return are
0x800362b4/0x800362b6. Event (category 4, code 26) subsequently reaches consumer
0x8002d568 -> 0x80008b68, which stops, unloads, reloads and saves again.
Join both worker and consumer, with actual I/O outcomes. Identical events and
a shared busy flag are not unique completion acknowledgements; one reload's
consumer can clear busy while another reload is still queued.

Do not park Main on a new blocked request if an earlier accepted reload needs
Main to consume its completion. The eventual caller-level deferral/retry ABI
must resolve this dependency before a gate can remain closed across a join.

5. Card and USB transitions
Outer candidates 0x8000c220/0x8000c288/0x8000c2d8 include side effects before
low-level USB requests. Independent setup 0x80009a40, release 0x80009b18 and
directory route 0x80009938 also matter. The inventory finds six direct detach
references and additional direct mount callers; guarding two USB wrappers
would omit known paths. Admission at detach 0x80062230 is already too late
for side effects in some enclosing workflows.

USB request 0x8003b7e0 and stop 0x8003b8a0 use one shared command/parameter pair
and untagged acknowledgement. The worker at 0x80045cf0 acknowledges before the
host-access session ends, including tested failed transition outcomes. Retain
the entire host session, verified stop, card readiness and reload descendants.
Neither the USB-active byte nor a zero-returning remount proves readiness.

Card release clears filesystem registration before pad/recorder cleanup. A
physical card removal cannot be prevented by software admission: invalidate
the card generation and retain failed/uncertain operations; never approve the
handoff from a stale idle/ready flag.

Pause ordering and release contract
1. Identify and close new mutation/producer admissions, including the outer
   caller ABI and lifetime of deferred arguments. Distinguish needed stops and
   accepted completions from new work. Keep interrupts and service workers live.
2. Join accepted control requests, renderer invocations, scan/refill/file work,
   recorder postprocessing, and storage/reload sessions using matched evidence.
   Logical ownership may persist; scratch/filesystem/SD locks must not.
3. Only then return gate OK and run seal, synchronous assignment and persistence.
4. Release with the I/O-free commit contract in backing_native.h. A failure must
   leave admissions closed; no failure may be reported after users resume.

The existing BackingGate.enter BUSY/SAFE contract leaves no reservations behind.
A future multi-step closer therefore cannot silently keep partial admissions
closed while returning BUSY: it needs a separate, explicit preparation state or
must unwind the attempt. Prefer trying the handoff at an already quiet boundary
and retaining the verified take when entry is unavailable. No implementation
choice is permitted to substitute idle flags for the matched evidence above.

Next implementation slice
Update: pad_commands_findings.txt records the completed offline queue and
three-argument caller evidence. Dispatcher ownership, retries, overflow and
ordering of mode/Record state changes still need binding. The remaining goals
below describe that integration, not a deployed gate.
Establish the two outer pad controls' actual caller contexts and deferral ABI,
including both start and stop branches and the Main reload dependency. Prove
that a delayed start preserves its arguments and executes once after release,
while a previously accepted completion can still progress. Keep the broad
research overlay excluded; do not wire BackingGate.enter to any existing flag
or capture-drain acknowledgement. The other domains remain explicit blockers
to enabling an actual device gate, not implicit assumptions of that slice.

Evidence and limits
New: five groups in verify_handoff_admission.py. Existing focused suites rerun:
control_drain, storage_lifetimes, pad_reload, native_scan, native_renderer_fence.
All 66 groups across these six suites passed.
Original instruction paths execute with modeled tasks, queue scheduling,
file/audio effects and admission boundaries. No complete firmware scheduler,
exhaustive caller graph, installed gate, new firmware image or device access.
