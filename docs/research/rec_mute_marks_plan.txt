Marking channels to leave out of a take: hold REC, tap MUTE (plan, 19 October 2026)
=================================================================================

A plan, not a result. Nothing here has been built, emulated or tried on the
mixer. It builds on docs/research/unused_track_files_findings.txt (the
stock recorder's file creation, section 1; the panel, section 3) and on
experiment 43 (the "muted with no sends" rule as a setting, on the branch
of pull request 2, not merged).

The workflow
------------
Hold REC. While it is held, tap the MUTE button of each channel to leave
out of the take; its MUTE light blinks slowly. Tap again to put it back.
Release REC: the take starts, the marked channels get no track file, and
their MUTE lights keep blinking until the take stops. A take started
without marks records everything. MASTER.WAV is always written. Nothing
carries over to the next take.

What this changes against experiment 43: the decision is made per take,
by hand, and shown while it matters, instead of a standing rule read from
the mutes. The rule and its AUX 2 + EFX setting can go, or stay as a second
option; the plan below assumes they go, which keeps one patch site.

Stock facts the plan rests on
-----------------------------
- REC (key 31) does nothing on press; its release runs the key handler
  0x80008a28, which starts or stops the take through the record request
  0x80034f40(-1). The only stock reader of REC's live state is the pad
  handler (0x800409a6 reads 0x80008478(31)): REC held plus a pad records
  onto that pad.
- MUTE 1-6 (keys 0-5) act on press: the dispatcher case 0x8002ca60 calls
  0x8003ed28(channel, 0, 0, pressed), which toggles the channel mute
  (0x8003e778: both lanes, stored with 0x80006658, applied with 0x80002928,
  the MUTE LED written with 0x80007a40) unless AUX 1, AUX 2 or EFX is held,
  when it mutes that send instead (held byte CONFIG+0x2311).
- The MUTE LEDs are the first six entries of the LED table 0x8062939c, two
  bytes each (level, mode; mode 1 blinks at the LED task's rate), written
  under the LED lock 0x80446840.
- The record prepare 0x80002df8 chooses the stream mask by calling
  0x80008990(target) at 0x80002e96; experiment 43 replaces that call with
  tm_select, which filters the mask. The stock start, stop, postprocess,
  loader and player handle a take with missing files as they handle Master
  Only (findings, section 1).
- Our stop setter hook (mr_stop, on RecStop's call of 0x80006918) runs at
  the end of every ordinary take; our admission hook (mr_admit) at its
  start. Experiment 43's admission test accepts a subset of streams.
- The panel reports keys in banks: REC is bank 3 bit 5; MUTE 1-6 are bank 1
  bits 1-6. Experiment 37 found that while a key is held, a press at the
  same bit of another bank is often not reported (PAN with LEVEL held, both
  bit 7, lost in 6 of 10 attempts), and the emulation's panel model
  reproduces that. REC and MUTE 5 share bit 5.

Design
------
State (zeroed globals, like panel.c's): marks, a six-bit mask of channels
marked for the next take; armed, set while REC is held and no take runs;
taken, the marks applied to the running take, for the blink and the stop.

1. The mark. A hook on 0x8003ed28's entry (an adapter in master_hooks.S,
   as the stock sites have; the displaced instructions are its prologue).
   When REC's live state reads 1, no recording runs or finalizes
   (panel_recording()), the pressed argument is 1 and no send key is held:
   flip the channel's bit in marks, write its MUTE LED (blink on, or back
   to the channel's mute with 0x80007a40), and return without the stock
   toggle. In every other case fall through to stock. A MUTE release is
   ignored by stock and by us.
   Alternative without a new site: Main's receive hook already sees every
   panel event as {0, press or release, key} and could turn a MUTE press
   with REC held into a code the dispatcher ignores; whether the default
   branch at 0x8002ce28 is silent was not checked. The adapter is cleaner.
2. The take. tm_select (the existing replaced call) clears both lane bits
   of every marked channel for an ordinary target, copies marks into taken
   and clears marks. For a pad target (REC held plus a pad) it returns
   stock's mask and clears marks.
3. The blink. While marks or taken is nonzero the marked channels' MUTE
   LEDs blink. Slow blink: the LED task's own blink is fixed and fast, so
   the worker toggles the level byte of each marked LED every 500 ms under
   the LED lock, from its 25 ms poll (panel_poll already runs there). The
   stock mode byte stays 0, so a stock redraw (a scene recall, a view
   press) shows the mute steady and our next toggle blinks it again.
4. The end. In mr_stop (RecStop), clear taken; the worker's next poll
   redraws the MUTE LEDs from the channel mutes with 0x80007a40. Marks made
   and never used (REC released onto a pad, or power off) are cleared at
   the pad target and at start-up (zeroed globals).
5. Edges. REC held while a take runs: the release will stop it, so MUTE
   stays stock (panel_recording() refuses the mark). A MUTE press with a
   send key and REC both held: the send mute, as stock. The stop setter
   hook is RecStop's only; PlayStop's call does not clear taken, and a
   pad recording's stop does not reach it, so taken must also clear on
   admission of the next take (mr_admit).
6. The setting. Removed with the rule, together with the AUX 2 + EFX pair
   in panel.c and the CONFIG+0x311 bytes; the build flag TRACK_MASK stays
   for the mask filter and the marks.

Code: about 60 lines in track_mask.c, a hook adapter, 20 lines in the
worker's poll, the mr_stop and mr_admit clears. One patch site more than
experiment 43 (16 in MAIN, plus the two DSP sites).

The panel question, first
-------------------------
If the panel drops MUTE 5 while REC is held, channel 5 cannot be marked and
the workflow is broken for one channel in six. Measure before building:

1. Install the experiment 37 build (build_master_trial.py panel_diag): it
   logs every key change the panel reports and the status query reads the
   log back (tools/device/l6_padref_probe.py schema 6, pd_ring).
2. Hold REC; tap MUTE 1 to 6 three times each, slowly; release REC. Repeat
   with MUTE 5 pressed before REC. Read the log: every tap must appear as a
   press and a release while key 31 is down.
3. Reinstall 0.3.0.

If REC + MUTE 5 is lost: the fallback is a modifier in the MUTE buttons'
own bank. LEVEL (bank 1 bit 7) held plus MUTE, which the stock parser
already treats as a combination for the AUX and EFX keys (held byte 8):
the mark hook acts when the held byte reads LEVEL, no live key read, and
the view ends on LEVEL, which changes nothing. Then REC alone starts the
take. The workflow reads worse but every channel is reachable.

Emulator suites
---------------
Extend tests/emulation/verify_track_mask.py (the rigs exist):
- The mark hook through the dispatcher's MUTE case with REC's live state
  modeled: a tap marks and blinks, a second tap unmarks and redraws from
  the mute, a tap without REC toggles the mute as stock, a tap with a send
  key held mutes the send, a tap while recording toggles the mute.
- The REC release through the stock key handler and record request (the
  existing group) with marks: the mask drops the marked channels, taken
  holds them, marks clear; a pad target keeps 0xc00 and clears the marks.
- The take through the original start, stop, postprocess, loader and
  preload (the existing group) with marked subsets; the admission and the
  stop clear taken; the chain and the history unaffected.
- The blink from the worker's poll: 500 ms on and off under the LED lock,
  steady from the mute after the stop, a stock redraw in between handled.
- The panel's measured key reporting (verify_stereo_aux_hooks.py's Panel):
  REC held and each MUTE tapped, in both orders, with the same-bit loss
  modeled, so the suite says what the diagnostic will say.
- The random-state group becomes marks against the model.

Hardware
--------
One experiment after the diagnostic: takes with marks on each channel,
PLAY on the mixer, the Editor's view of the card, the chain and
HISTORY.TXT, a take with no marks, a pad recording with REC held, power
off with marks pending. About a day with the write-up.

Open questions
--------------
1. Keep the "muted with no sends" rule as a second option, or drop it?
2. Blink rate: the LED task's own rate (no worker work) or 500 ms?
3. Should the marked channels' lights blink for the whole take, or only
   while REC is held?
4. If REC + MUTE 5 fails on the panel, LEVEL + MUTE, or REC + MUTE for
   channels 1-4 and 6 with channel 5 marked some other way?
