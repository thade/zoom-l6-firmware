Remaining limits
The sustained stock recording/pad workload and both loop joins now have saved
audio evidence. Diagnostic queries measure native return times,
not physical card completion. Public read/write hooks include lock/scheduling
waits but do not cover every open, seek, close or finalizer operation. Skipped
calls must remain visible; an observed maximum cannot establish a guaranteed
worker blackout bound. Extra capture throughput, starting history age, catch-up,
largest contiguous allocation, later stock reserve and task/IRQ stack headroom
remain unmeasured. Keep 128 history slots only as a conditional candidate.
