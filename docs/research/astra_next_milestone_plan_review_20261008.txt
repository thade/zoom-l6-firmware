Recommendation
--------------
The direction is right. Implementing and testing a larger audio history is the
next coding milestone; making the extra recorder safe to deploy is a subsequent
integration milestone. The four-step plan made the hardware trial sound closer
than the evidence supports.

Start fixed segmented history offline now, alongside a finite audit of its exact
memory spans. Do not default to another broad round of guard-only experiments.
Keep the contiguous backend, one producer, one consumer, separate worker and
aligned 4-KiB staging. No new queue, scheduler or pad machinery is necessary.

Material findings
-----------------
1. The simulation-to-device step omitted concrete bindings.

   Worker creation/release, executable and permanent-global placement, storage
   admission and physical transfer completion remain unfinished. Reclaimed
   history tails do not establish ownership of candidate code/global addresses.
   Use heap-owned small runtime objects with external history, while verifying
   executable/global placement separately. Recalculate allocation and packed
   image margins after composition.

   native_worker.h:35 explicitly requires verified storage admission before
   release and states there is no production caller. build_extra_capture.py:1
   still builds offline ELFs; line 21 prohibits combining heap and startup/
   placement fixtures. A mount flag or valid Main identity is not storage
   readiness. capture_storage_findings.txt:69 describes teardown invalidating
   metadata during live IO. capture_io_lifetime_findings.txt:16 still relies on
   a modeled completion provider, including nominal success.

2. Replace "nothing else uses this memory" with a bounded access contract.

   The recent effects test qualifies checked writes through exercised native
   effect paths. It does not exercise SD activity or the additional capture tap,
   or establish reads, aliases, cache/DMA ownership.

   Inventory the exact eight proposed tails: initialization/clearing/reentry;
   both descriptors and cached capacities; direct/computed audio and effects
   accesses; native file/DMA source spans; transitions restoring access to tails.
   Each entry ends with a supported bound, an exclusion enforced before admission,
   or a named unresolved dependency. Intact guards support the audit but cannot
   answer read/alias questions. Offline implementation can proceed alongside
   this inventory without claiming hardware ownership.

   Evidence: ring_probe_findings.txt:365 and :394.

3. Treat 1024 slots as prototype geometry, not a service guarantee.

   Eight tails of 128 slots fit the tested 223104-frame ordinary layout and hold
   1.365 seconds of extra history. Use this geometry for the offline candidate
   rather than inventing another layout immediately (native_storage_layout_
   findings.txt:121).

   The 481-ms measurement is a filesystem held interval; 962 ticks is an
   unattributed take interval. Neither measures the extra worker's blackout
   (service_probe_findings.txt:191). Budget existing history age, time until the
   next successful copy, repeated stalls and recovery throughput, including the
   worker's deliberate sleep after each batch (native_worker.c:106).

   Normal initialized recording selects no lookback, although setup delays the
   first consumer access. The latent 0.5/2-second branches are not established
   user modes; reject unsupported ranges when encountered (record_boundaries_
   findings.txt:24). Sampled ordinary modulo backlog is not a worst-case bound.

   Preserve the separate worker. A controlled delayed final-stream schedule
   reproduces premature native staging reuse (native_storage_layout_findings.
   txt:51); inserting extra work there is not a justified simplification. Shared
   storage contention still requires actual scheduling/service measurement.

4. Keep signal routing and failure behavior in the acceptance criteria.

   Ordinary USB/master effect responses do not prove effects exist at the
   extra tap. Verify live-input and pad inclusion, effects behavior, and separation
   from master dynamics at that tap (architecture.md:21). Begin offline; this
   does not require reopening the deferred level investigation.

   Missing history can invalidate only the extra take while ordinary recording
   continues. An uncertain physical transfer can retain shared filesystem
   resources and stall ordinary users (capture_io_lifetime_findings.txt:107).
   Do not promise every failure affects only the extra file before the physical
   completion binding supports that claim.

Acceptance
----------
* Reject invalid, overlapping, undersized, misaligned and wrapping spans before
  publication; never touch the gaps between tails.
* Preserve exact slot identity and samples across all segment boundaries and
  repeated whole-ring wraps, including producer/reader claim races.
* Preserve one exact extra file and all seven ordinary files through compiled
  lifecycle tests: partial first/last blocks, delayed admission, stop, continued
  audio after stop, and rearm.
* Recover from modeled repeated stalls that fit the candidate history. Excess
  stalls, missing generations and a stop endpoint behind already-copied audio
  must yield no eligible take.
* Stop does not freeze the producer. Selected endpoint samples survive until
  copied, or the extra take fails (emulator_bridge.c:279).
* Update heap/packing figures and run focused regressions for changed protocols.
  Worker timing settings remain experimental.
