Panel keys, LEDs and a second hold switch (stock L6 v1.10, 16 October 2026)
==========================================================================

Research for a panel control that turns the take chain on and off, next to the
AUX 1 + AUX 2 switch (aux_switch_findings.txt). Sections 1-5: static reading of
the stock image (capstone). Section 6: hardware. Built first as a PAN + LEVEL
hold (experiment 35); because the panel often lost PAN while LEVEL was held
(section 6), 0.1.0 uses EFX + PAN (experiment 38).

1. Key indices of the live key read 0x80008478 (1 pressed, 0 released, 2 none)
Panel-MCU keys read bit/word pairs from the table 0x800a153c (words
0x808cb5fc/600/604, 0x808cb608 for keys 8-11) through 0x800399c8; Main-MCU keys
read the active-low GPIO copy [0x8060e3a4] through 0x8001fca0. Each index equals
the Main event code; the dispatcher 0x8002c9d8 logs a name for every code, and
the boot chooser's combinations agree (HIGH+FREQ 12+13, REC 31, A 27, A+B+C
27-29, USB 1/2 + pad 1-3 = 10 + 21-23).
  0-5 MUTE ch 1-6       6 COMP              7 PHANTOM (+48 V)
  8, 9 MONO ch 3, 4     10, 11 USB 1/2, 3/4 12-15 HIGH, FREQ, MID, LOW
  16, 17 AUX1, AUX2     18 EFX              19 PAN       20 LEVEL
  21-24 SOUND PAD 1-4 (GPIO)  25 TAP (GPIO)  26 SEL       27-29 scene A-C
  30 no hardware        31 REC              32 PLAY/STOP (GPIO)
  33 second PWR_SW code, no source          34 POWER (GPIO)

2. Two-button holds at run time
Releases cancel long-press timers (0x8001fb80; inline for pads and TAP in the
GPIO poller 0x8006d470). Only pads (800 ms: stop), scenes (1 s: save), PLAY
(800 ms, ignored) and POWER (800 ms: power-off check) have one. No stock code
combines keys; the only run-time reader of the live state is the pad handler
(REC held, 0x800409a6). On press: MUTE, COMP, PHANTOM, MONO and USB toggle;
TAP sets the tempo; SEL cycles the effect; PLAY starts or stops playback; pads
play. REC acts on release (starts or stops a take). Scene release recalls.
The view keys 12-20 (handler 0x8003dd00) set the view byte CONFIG+0x2310
(0x804430d8) and the held byte +0x2311, and redraw the select, encoder-ring and
MUTE LEDs; release (0x8003d588) clears the held byte. The held byte matters
only for AUX1, AUX2 and EFX (MUTE then mutes that send). Nothing audible and
nothing saved: 0x2310/0x2311 lie outside the saved global block. So PAN +
LEVEL (19 + 20) is free and harmless; the view ends on the button pressed
second. HIGH + FREQ is avoided (setting mode at power-on).

3. Power-on
At the plain normal-boot site 0x8000870c the mask is in r4 (key k = bit k) and
r5 (keys 32-34; POWER must be masked). Every mask boots normally there except
the exact stock combinations and A+B+C with extra keys. Panel keys held at
power-on arrive in the panel MCU's state dump without a press event; a later
release is posted (harmless for view keys). A pad held at power-on may be
reported as a press by the first GPIO poll (debounce state starts zeroed,
0x8001f7c0): unverified, so never use a pad at power-on.

4. LEDs (table 0x8062939c, 2 bytes each: level, mode)
  0-5 MUTE (0x80007a40)   6 COMP   7 +48 V   8, 9 MONO   10, 11 USB (0x80007e60)
  12-20 select LEDs, index sel+0xc with sel = key - 12 (0x800075d8)
  21 REC (0x80007c20)     22 PLAY (0x80007ad0)   29-31 scenes (0x80007d18)
  44-47 pads (0x80007c90) 48-101 encoder rings; 24-28 and 32-43 probable
  effect and channel indicators (medium confidence)
The LED task near 0x8002b1ee sends only changes to the panel MCU, which does
the blinking. Level 0x10 full, 2 dim; mode 0 steady, 1 blink. Pad LEDs are
written by the 32 ms task 0x80037288 through 0x8004af10 only when a pad's state
changes (cache 0x801f8ecc+5+pad), so a temporary pattern would persist until
then. REC/PLAY LEDs have no cached state to restore. A view press redraws all
nine select LEDs from the view byte, which is what the switches' redraw does.

5. Storage for a flag
Spare global bytes CONFIG+0x30f..0x31c and 0x36f remain (0x30d/0x30e hold the
AUX mode; 0x30f/0x310 now hold the chain mode). Per-pad fields at CONFIG+0xfc0..
and the pad path structs are rebuilt from L6PADSETTING.ZST and never reach
internal flash; CONFIG+0x329..0x32c (unique per pad, likely MIDI notes) and
0x32d+i are in use.

6. Key groups and two-button holds on the mixer (experiments 36-37)
Each panel key is a bit in one of the panel words (the table 0x800a153c gives
bank and bit): bank 2 bits 0-7 are HIGH FREQ MID LOW AUX1 AUX2 EFX PAN; bank 1
bits 0-7 are +48V MUTE1-6 LEVEL; bank 0 bits 1-4 MONO3 MONO4 USB12 USB34;
bank 3 bits 0-5 SEL COMP SCENE A B C REC; pads, TAP, PLAY and POWER are GPIO.
The parser 0x800393d0 sets a key's bit on a press message, clears it on a
release message, and rewrites all words from the panel controller's 0xF0
state dump; nothing else writes them. A diagnostic build (experiment 37)
logged every change at 25 ms: in 6 of 10 PAN + LEVEL attempts PAN never
appeared while LEVEL was held (LEVEL alone for 4-10 s; 4,596 polls with one
key against 746 with both), while 4 of 4 AUX 1 + AUX 2 attempts registered
both keys. PAN and LEVEL are bit 7 of two different banks; AUX 1 and AUX 2
are adjacent bits of one bank. The cause lies in the panel controller (its
own firmware); same-bank pairs are reliable, so the chain uses EFX + PAN.
The same build measured Main's first return from a receive at 2.99 s after
the scheduler started (the worker's first poll at 7 ms, the first gain
request at 31 ms), so the switches now arm 1 s after Main is ready.
